# Problem Statement

## Problem Identified

Food delivery system

## Background

A Food Delivery System is needed to make the ordering and delivery process faster, organized, and convenient. The system can provide online menu browsing, easy order placement, secure payment, real-time order tracking, and delivery-status updates. It can also help restaurants manage orders efficiently and help delivery partners handle deliveries systematically.

## Proposed Solution

The proposed Food Delivery System is an online platform that connects customers, restaurants, and delivery partners in a single system. Customers can browse restaurants and menus, select food items, place orders, make payments, and track their orders. Restaurants can manage menus, receive and process orders, and update order status. Delivery partners can view assigned orders and update delivery progress.

## Objective

develop an effective Food Delivery System that simplifies and manages the entire food ordering and delivery process. The system aims to provide customers with an easy way to browse menus, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries, while helping restaurants and delivery partners manage orders efficiently.
